The
Story

Manitoba Ag Days is the largest indoor agriculture show in Canada. Over three days in Brandon, Manitoba, thousands of farmers, manufacturers, dealers, and industry leaders converge on one floor to talk product, shake hands, and do business. The energy is relentless — and it only happens once a year.

Our full production team was on-site from day one, embedded in the rhythm of the event. We covered the trade floor as it moved — booth conversations that turned into deals, keynote addresses that set the agenda for the year ahead, and the quiet moments between sessions where real connections happen.

The brief was clear: capture everything, miss nothing. We deployed multiple camera operators simultaneously so no story was left uncovered. From wide establishing shots of the packed venue to intimate one-on-one interviews with exhibitors, attendees, and organizers, we built a visual record of what makes Ag Days one of the most important events in the Canadian agricultural calendar.

The deliverables were built for multiple audiences — a full event recap film for the organizers, exhibitor-specific highlight reels for individual booths, and a library of social-first content that kept the conversation going long after the doors closed.

What We Delivered

01

Event Recap Film

A cinematic 5–7 minute recap capturing the full arc of the event — from opening day arrivals to the energy of the final session — built to live on the Ag Days website and YouTube channel.

02

Exhibitor Highlights

Individual 60–90 second highlight reels for select exhibitors, giving them professional footage of their booth, conversations, and products to use in their own marketing after the show.

03

Social Content Library

A full suite of short-form vertical and horizontal cuts for Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn — interview clips, crowd moments, and product showcases formatted for immediate posting.
