Privacy policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026. This policy explains how Three Point Studios (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects personal information through threepointstudios.ca. We follow the principles of Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • Inquiry details. When you fill out our inquiry form or contact us, we collect what you choose to share, such as your name, company, email address, phone number and details about your project.
  • Project details. If you work with us, we keep the information needed to plan and deliver the project, such as timelines, contacts, agreements and payment records.
  • Usage data. Our site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and records things like pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to your inquiry, prepare quotes, plan and deliver your project, send invoices and keep records required by law. We use analytics data to understand which pages are useful and to improve the site. We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and remarketing

We use Google Analytics and advertising tools from Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) to measure which pages and ads lead to inquiries, and to show our ads to people who have visited this site. These tools use cookies and similar technologies. Google may connect visits across your devices if you are signed in to a Google account with ad personalization turned on. When you submit an inquiry form, these tools are told that a form was sent; the answers you type stay with us.

You can turn off personalized ads in Google ad settings and in your Meta ad preferences, install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or block cookies in your browser.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers we need to run our business and deliver your project, for example our CRM, form, email and file-delivery providers, payment processors and the team members or contractors working on your project. These providers may store data outside Canada and may only use it to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information where required by law.

How long we keep it

Inquiries that do not lead to a project are kept only as long as useful to follow up, then deleted. Project and financial records are kept as long as needed to deliver the work and as required for tax and legal purposes.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or withdraw consent to further use (subject to legal or contractual limits) by emailing hello@tridenteventgroup.ca.
